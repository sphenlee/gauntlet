Magic
-----

There are 4 classes of magic: green, blue, red, arcane

Many spells come in multiple levels of strength. They use a common root
with suffixes to indicate the level. The second level spell has the same strength as
first level, but targets all enemies in range. The fourth level is the same as third, but
targets all enemies in range.

Green Magic
-----------

Green magic is earth and water elemental, with healing and protective spells.

* Terra/Terris/Terara/Terrom: earth element damage
* Aqua/Aquis/Aquara/Aquom: water element damage
* Cure/Curara/Curom: restore health (25%, 50% and 100%)
* Shell: increase defense by 1.5x for 30s
* Arise: restore KO character to 25% health

Blue Magic
----------

Blue magic is air and ice elemental, with status effects (beneficial for
the party and harmful for enemies).

* Gale/Galis/Galara/Galom: air element damage
* Frost/Frostis/Frostara/Frostom: ice element damage
* Darkness: reduces an enemy's accuracy to 0 for 30s
* Sleep: stops an enemy from performing any actions for 30s (or until hit by a weapon attack)

Red Magic
---------

Red magic is lightning and fire elemental, with purely offensive spells.

* Bolt/Boltis/Boltara/Boltom: lightning element damage
* Pyre/Pyris/Pyrara/Pyrom: fire element damage
* Astra/Astris/Astrara/Astrom: non-elemental damage

Arcane Magic
------------

Arcane magic is the top tier magic,

* Light: ultimate non-elemental damage
